## How the work is run

Engagements are short and principal-led. There is no leverage model here — the
person you meet in the first conversation writes the analysis and sits in the
board session. That constrains volume, and it is the point.

A typical mandate runs six to ten weeks:

- **Weeks one to two.** Interviews with the executive team, the operators two
  levels down, and, where the mandate allows, customers. Financial baseline
  rebuilt from source systems rather than from the management pack.
- **Weeks three to six.** The analytical core. Two or three genuine options,
  costed, with the conditions under which each fails stated plainly.
- **Weeks seven onward.** A recommendation, a decision paper the board can act
  on, and — where it is wanted — presence in the room when the decision is
  taken.

## Where we work

Industrial manufacturing, medical technology, specialty chemicals, and
mid-market financial services. Clients are typically European groups with
revenue between €200m and €4bn, and the private equity houses that own them.

## What we decline

Work that exists to ratify a decision already made. Diligence where the
timetable does not permit a defensible answer. Any mandate where the
recommendation is written before the analysis.
