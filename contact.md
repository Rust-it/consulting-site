## What is useful in a first message

A short note is enough. Three things make the first conversation more
productive:

- **The decision, not the topic.** "Whether to keep the components division"
  is workable. "Portfolio strategy" is not yet a question.
- **Who has to be convinced.** A board, an executive committee, a sponsor, or
  a single sceptical director — each implies a different piece of work.
- **The date the decision has to be taken.** If the timetable does not allow a
  defensible answer, it is better to say so at the outset than at week four.

## Availability

The practice runs two engagements at a time. Capacity is usually committed six
to eight weeks ahead; urgent mandates are occasionally accommodated by
declining something else.

## Press

Journalists are welcome to make contact by email. Comment is given on
attributable record only, and not on matters concerning current or recent
clients.
